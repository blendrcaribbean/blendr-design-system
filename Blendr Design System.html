✓

Do keep proportions fixed

Scale uniformly; preserve the wave tail on the N.

✓

Do use on approved palette

Ink, Foam, Sky, Paper. Always check contrast on imagery.

✕

Don't outline or emboss

The mark is a solid fill, no strokes or shadows.

✕

Don't tint or gradient

Solid brand colors only. No gradients on the wordmark.

✕

Don't place on busy imagery

Use a brand background or a solid panel behind it.

✕

Don't re-stack the wordmark

BLENDR stays horizontal. "Branding & Design" always sits beneath.

✕

Don't pair dark on dark

Never place the Ink/Deep Teal wordmark on a dark surface. Use the reversed Foam mark instead. And never put the light mark on Paper/Mist.

✕

Don't rely on low contrast

The wordmark must always clear at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against its background.

04 | Waves & Patterns

Waves carry
the composition.

The Blendr wave library ranges from quiet single-arc marks to dense stacked compositions. Choose by volume: busy content needs quiet waves; clean content can carry a bold one.

Stacked | Hero

Three-layer composition for covers, headers, and key moments. High visual weight.

Ribbon | Quiet

Single curved ribbon. Use behind long-form content and editorial layouts.

Frame | Bracketing

Top and bottom waves frame a central canvas. Great for text-heavy slides.

Waves · as code

A reusable wave system.

The library ships as a single script, assets/waves.js, exposing an SVG generator, a <blendr-wave> web component, and palette tokens. Use it to animate covers, theme slides on the fly, or carry the brand into new surfaces.
